How elevators work

A comprehensive technical overview of drive types, engineering mechanics, and building standards.

Drive Systems

Four drive mechanisms

Traction (Gearless Permanent Magnet)

Suspension ropes run over a synchronously driven motorized sheave. A counterweight offsets 50% of the passenger load, meaning the drive only lifts net differences. This delivers the highest efficiency and smoothest ride profile for mid-to-high rise buildings.

  • Optimal for 5 to 40+ floors
  • Lowest power draw per trip
  • Quiet high-speed operation (up to 4.0 m/s)

Machine-Room-Less (MRL)

The compact drive motor is mounted directly to the head of the hoistway guide rails, removing any need for a rooftop penthouse machine room. This eliminates structural roof loads and saves valuable architectural volume.

  • No rooftop room required
  • Cuts civil building expenses
  • Perfect for private villas and residential blocks

Hydraulic Piston

A heavy-duty hydraulic ram driven by pressurized fluid elevates the cabin from the foundation pit. Ideal for low-rise industrial structures carrying heavy freight, machinery, and vehicles.

  • Unmatched load capacity (up to 5,000 kg)
  • Minimal overhead clearance needed
  • Heavy-duty industrial reliability

Pneumatic Vacuum Lift

Operates using atmospheric differential pressure inside a clear polycarbonate tube. Requires zero pit excavation and no machine shaft, fitting directly into existing duplexes and penthouses.

  • Zero pit or machine shaft required
  • 360-degree panoramic glass views
  • Fast 2-day installation

FAQ

Common questions

What occurs during a sudden mains power cut?

Every LEO elevator comes standard with an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD). Within 5 seconds of an outage, an auxiliary battery bank activates, powers the motor to the nearest floor, and opens the doors automatically.

What civil preparation is required for a home lift?

A shallow pit of 300 mm to 1200 mm (depending on model) and an overhead clearance of 2800 mm to 3400 mm on the top floor. For zero-pit applications, our pneumatic and platform lifts install directly on finished floor tiles.

How often does an elevator need maintenance under law?

Indian State Lift Regulations mandate regular monthly preventative servicing by an authorized OEM partner along with an annual safety inspection and load test.

Is GST included in the equipment quote?

Our online calculator displays both the base cost and 18% GST itemized transparently for straightforward tax accounting.

Home / Safety & AMC

Multi-layered safety architecture

Every LEO lift integrates active optical barriers, progressive mechanical brakes, and smart telemetry.

Overspeed Governor

A centrifugal safety trigger locks steel wedges against the rail beams if descent speed exceeds 115% of rated limit.

Auto Rescue Device (ARD)

Auxiliary battery banks safely maneuver the car to the nearest landing level and release passengers during blackouts.

Infrared Light Curtains

An array of 128 infrared cross-beams across the doorway instantly reopens the doors before physical contact.

Load Cell Sensors

Monitors cabin load. If passengers exceed max capacity, a buzzer sounds and doors remain open until cleared.
